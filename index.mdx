GPR (GetPullRequest) orchestrates your entire development workflow—from picking up a Jira or Linear ticket to opening a verified pull request on GitHub or GitLab—using AI coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor running on your own machine. Monitor progress, review diffs, and merge, all from your phone.

## How It Works

GPR ties together the tools you already use into one automated loop—so your coding agents do the coding while you stay in control.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assign a Task">
    Pick a Jira ticket, Linear issue, or create a task directly in GPR. Assign it to a coding agent running on your local machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent Does the Work">
    Your coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any ACP-compatible agent) runs in your real development environment—with access to your actual codebase, tools, and tests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="GPR Verifies the Changes">
    Before opening a pull request, GPR runs your tests and checks in the real environment to confirm the changes are correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Merge from Your Phone">
    Get notified on Slack or in the GPR mobile app. Review the diff, approve, and merge—without touching your laptop.
  </Step>
</Steps>
